Step 2

Analyze Image Characteristics

The system measures key characteristics of the input image before selecting an enhancement method.

Mean Brightness
--
YCrCb luminance [0–255]
Indicates the overall illumination level of the image.
RMS Contrast
--
Global standard deviation
Represents the global variation in pixel intensity.
Dynamic Range
--
P95 - P5 intensity span
Represents the spread between darker and brighter regions.
Noise Level
--
Immerkaer Laplacian estimate
Estimates high-frequency image sensor noise.
Illumination Variation
--
Low-frequency CV gradient
Measures spatial variation in illumination across the image.
